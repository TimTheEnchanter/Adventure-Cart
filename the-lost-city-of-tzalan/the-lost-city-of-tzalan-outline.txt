# The Lost City of Tzalan: a long, fair adventure for a 64K cartridge
Title: The Lost City of Tzalan
Cart: 64K
Maps: 8
Start: Base camp
Ends when: City awakened

# ---------------- the jungle ----------------
Base camp
  Your camp sits in a jungle clearing. Insects buzz.
  Look: Tents and a crackling campfire. A machete is stuck in a stump.
  Item: a machete (look)
  Take: You pull the machete free.
  Light: You hold the reeds in the campfire. They catch! Now you have a torch. [needs a reed torch] [uses it up] [gives a lit torch] [refused: The campfire crackles. You have nothing to light.] [done: The fire crackles happily.]
  North: Jungle path
  East: River landing
  West: Reed marsh
  South: Supply tent

Supply tent
  You are in the supply tent.
  Look: Empty crates. One corn cake is left in a tin.
  Item: a corn cake (look)
  Take: You wrap up the corn cake.

Reed marsh
  You squelch through a reed marsh.
  Look: Tall dry reeds, good for a torch. The mud to the west bubbles.
  Item: a reed torch (look)
  Take: You bind a bundle of reeds into a torch. It is not lit.
  West: Quicksand
  North: Fig tree

Quicksand
  The mud sucks at your boots. You sink... and sink...
  Ending: game over

River landing
  You are at the river landing.
  Look: Your canoe is tied up here. A coil of rope lies in it.
  Item: some rope (look)
  Take: You sling the rope over your shoulder.
  North: Riverbank

Riverbank
  You walk along a muddy riverbank.
  Look: A water gourd floats in the shallows.
  Item: a gourd (look)
  Take: You fill the gourd with fresh water.
  West: Jungle path
  North: Kapok tree

Kapok tree
  A giant kapok tree towers over you.
  Look: Its roots are like walls. You could climb it.
  Climb: From the top you see stone towers far to the north! [sets Saw the city] [done: Just jungle, and the towers to the north.]
  West: Overgrown trail

Jungle path
  You follow a narrow jungle path.
  Look: Strange birds call. The path runs north.
  North: Overgrown trail
  West: Fig tree

Fig tree
  You stand under a huge fig tree.
  Look: A cheeky monkey sits on a branch. Something shiny hangs on its collar.
  Talk: The monkey chatters and stares at your pack. It looks hungry.
  Give a corn cake: The monkey grabs the cake and drops the shiny thing! [sets Monkey fed]
  Item: a stone key
  Take: You pick up a carved stone key.
  West: Parrot clearing
  After Monkey fed, look: The monkey munches its cake, very pleased with itself.

Parrot clearing
  You are in a sunny clearing.
  Look: A red parrot watches you from a branch.
  Talk: The parrot squawks: "MOON BY NIGHT! SUN BY DAY! GOLD FOR THE GUARDIAN!"
  South: Stream

Overgrown trail
  You are on an overgrown trail.
  Look: Thick vines block the way north.
  Use a machete: You hack through the vines. The way north is clear! [sets Vines cut]
  North: Gorge edge (locked until Vines cut: Thick vines block the way.)
  After Vines cut, look: Cut vines lie everywhere. The way north is open.

Gorge edge
  You are at the edge of a deep gorge.
  Look: A rope bridge hangs in tatters. You could mend it with rope.
  Use some rope: You tie the rope across the gap. The bridge will hold! [sets Bridge mended] [uses it up]
  North: Far bank (locked until Bridge mended: The bridge is broken.)
  East: Waterfall
  After Bridge mended, look: Your mended bridge sways over the gorge.

Waterfall
  A waterfall roars into the gorge.
  Look: Wet rocks lead up behind the falling water.
  Climb: You climb behind the waterfall and find an old carving: a star above a throne. [sets Found the carving] [done: The carving of the star is still there.]
  North: Overlook

Far bank
  You are on the far bank of the gorge.
  Look: An old stone road leads north into the jungle.
  North: Stone road

Stone road
  You walk an ancient stone road.
  Look: Statues of jaguars line the way, green with moss.
  North: City gate
  East: Hunters' hide

Hunters' hide
  You find an old hunters' hide.
  Look: Bones and broken spears. Nobody has been here for centuries.
  East: Ruined shrine

City gate
  You stand before the gate of Tzalan.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: A huge stone door, carved with a keyhole.
  Use a stone key: The key turns. The great door grinds open! [sets Gate opened]
  North: Great plaza (locked until Gate opened: The gate is shut.)
  After Gate opened, look: The great door stands open.

# ---------------- the city ----------------
Great plaza
  You are in the great plaza of Tzalan.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Silent pyramids and towers all around. Nothing moves.
  North: Avenue of kings
  East: Market ruins
  West: Explorer's tent

Explorer's tent
  You find a ragged tent.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: An old explorer lies here, weak with thirst.
  Talk: "Water... please... water..."
  Give a gourd: He drinks deeply. "Bless you! Take my journal. It may help." [sets Explorer helped]
  Item: a journal
  Take: You take the old journal.
  Read: The journal says: THE MOON OPENS THE NIGHT TEMPLE. THE SUN OPENS THE DAY. [needs a journal] [sets Read the journal] [refused: You have nothing to read.]
  West: Ball court
  After Explorer helped, look: The explorer sleeps peacefully, his water gourd at his side.

Ball court
  You are in an ancient ball court.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Stone rings high on the walls. Glyphs run along the floor.
  Read: The glyphs say: THE GUARDIAN BELOW TRADES ONLY FOR GOLD. [sets Read the glyphs] [done: THE GUARDIAN TRADES ONLY FOR GOLD.]
  South: Bath house

Market ruins
  You are in the ruined market.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Broken stalls. An old knife lies among the pots.
  Item: an old knife (look)
  Take: You take the old knife. Still sharp!
  East: Observatory
  South: Old well

Observatory
  You stand at the foot of a round tower.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: The observatory. Its stairs have fallen, but you could climb the stones.
  Climb: You climb the crumbling tower. At the top lies a silver moon disc! [gives a moon disc] [done: The stars must have looked fine from here.]
  North: Aqueduct

Old well
  You are beside an old well.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: The well is very deep. A strong vine hangs from a tree nearby.
  Climb: You tie the vine to the well and test it. You can climb down now. [sets Vine tied] [done: The vine hangs down into the well.]
  South: Well bottom (passage; locked until Vine tied: The well is too deep to climb down.)

Avenue of kings
  You walk the Avenue of Kings.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Stone kings stare down. A temple stands west, a pyramid east.
  West: Moon temple
  East: Sun pyramid
  North: Priests' houses

Priests' houses
  You are among the priests' houses.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Painted walls show priests bowing to a glowing star.
  North: Library
  West: Garden of stone

Moon temple
  You stand before the Moon Temple.
  Colours: $82 $8E
  Look: A round hollow is carved in the door, the size of a disc.
  Use a moon disc: The disc fits! The temple door slides open. [sets Moon door open]
  West: Moon hall (locked until Moon door open: The temple door is sealed.)

Moon hall
  You are in the Moon Hall.
  Colours: $82 $8E
  Dark unless a lit torch: It is black as night in here.
  Look: Silver moons are painted on the walls.
  West: Moon sanctum

Moon sanctum
  You are in the Moon Sanctum.
  Colours: $82 $8E
  Dark unless a lit torch: You can't see a thing.
  Look: A golden sun disc rests on the altar. Odd, in a moon temple!
  Item: a sun disc (look)
  Take: You lift the heavy sun disc.

Sun pyramid
  You stand at the foot of the Sun Pyramid.
  Colours: $1A $10
  Look: Its door has a round hollow, the size of a disc.
  Use a sun disc: The disc fits! Stone grinds as the door opens. [sets Sun door open]
  North: Pyramid stairs (locked until Sun door open: The pyramid door is sealed.)

Pyramid stairs
  You climb the steep inner stairs.
  Colours: $1A $10
  Look: Hundreds of steps lead up to the sky.
  North: Pyramid summit

Pyramid summit
  You reach the top of the Sun Pyramid.
  Colours: $1A $10
  Look: An altar with the shape of a face carved into it. You can see for miles.
  Use a jade mask: You set the mask in the altar. Far below, water rushes away! [sets Lake drained] [uses it up]
  After Lake drained, look: The jade mask glows on the altar.

# ---------------- under the city ----------------
Well bottom
  You are at the bottom of the well.
  Dark unless a lit torch: Total darkness. Water drips.
  Look: A tunnel leads south.
  North: Old well
  South: Cavern

Cavern
  You are in a huge cavern.
  Dark unless a lit torch: Darkness all around. A cold wind blows from the south.
  Look: Tunnels lead west and east. A cold wind blows from the south.
  West: Web tunnel
  East: Lake shore
  South: Chasm

Chasm
  You step into empty air and fall into the chasm!
  Ending: game over

Web tunnel
  You are in a tunnel thick with webs.
  Dark unless a lit torch: Something sticky brushes your face.
  Look: Huge webs block the way west. You could cut them.
  Use an old knife: You slash the webs. The way west is clear! [sets Webs cut]
  West: Hidden vault (locked until Webs cut: Thick webs block the way.)

Hidden vault
  You are in a hidden vault.
  Colours: $C2 $CE
  Look: A beautiful jade mask lies on a stone shelf.
  Item: a jade mask (look)
  Take: You take the jade mask.
  North: Bone pit

Lake shore
  You stand at the shore of a dark lake.
  Colours: $90 $9E
  Look: Black water fills the cave. Something glints far below.
  After Lake drained: The lake has drained away! Steps lead down to the lake bed.
  After Lake drained, look: Wet steps lead down to the lake bed.
  South: Lake bed (locked until Lake drained: The water is far too deep.)
  East: Hall of masks

Lake bed
  You are on the muddy lake bed.
  Colours: $90 $9E
  Look: Among the mud and shells lies a gold idol!
  Item: a gold idol (look)
  Take: You take the gold idol.
  South: Crystal cave

Hall of masks
  You are in the Hall of Masks.
  Colours: $54 $5E
  Look: Stone faces line the walls. At the end, a ghostly figure waits.
  East: Guardian's hall
  North: Mushroom grotto

Guardian's hall
  You are in the Guardian's hall.
  Colours: $54 $5E
  Look: The ghost of an old warrior guards a gate of stars.
  Talk: "Who wakes me? Bring gold, and I will open the Gate of Stars."
  Give a gold idol: The guardian smiles. "A fine gift." The Gate of Stars swings open! [sets Guardian pleased]
  North: Star chamber (locked until Guardian pleased: The guardian bars the way.)
  After Guardian pleased, look: The guardian nods. The Gate of Stars stands open.

Star chamber
  You are in the Star Chamber.
  Colours: $00 $1E
  Look: Points of light glitter in the ceiling. A glowing stone rests on a pedestal.
  Item: a star stone (look)
  Take: The star stone is warm in your hand.
  North: Throne room

Throne room
  You are in the throne room of Tzalan.
  Colours: $00 $1E
  Look: A great stone throne, with a star-shaped hollow above it.
  Use a star stone: You set the star stone above the throne. Light floods the city. Tzalan awakens! [sets City awakened]

# ---------------- extra places ----------------
Stream
  You splash across a cool stream.
  Look: Bright frogs sit on the stones. One croaks at you.
  Talk: The frog croaks. You feel a little silly.

Ruined shrine
  You find a small ruined shrine.
  Look: Offerings of old shells lie before a broken idol.
  Read: The shrine says: TRAVELLER, THE CITY SLEEPS UNTIL THE STAR RETURNS.

Overlook
  You stand on a rocky overlook.
  Look: Mist rises from the gorge. Far north, towers poke through the trees.

Bath house
  You are in the old bath house.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Dry stone pools. Painted fish swim on the walls.

Aqueduct
  You walk along a crumbling aqueduct.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Once it carried water to the whole city. Now it carries leaves.

Library
  You are in the house of books.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Folded bark books, most turned to dust.
  Read: One book survives: THE KINGS SLEEP BELOW THE LAKE, GUARDED BY THE OLD WARRIOR.

Garden of stone
  You are in a garden of stone.
  Colours: $F2 $FE
  Look: Stone flowers and stone birds. Not a living thing grows here.

Mushroom grotto
  You are in a glowing grotto.
  Colours: $C0 $CC
  Look: Pale mushrooms give off a faint green light.

Crystal cave
  You are in a crystal cave.
  Colours: $90 $9E
  Look: Crystals sparkle in the lamplight like a sky full of stars.

Bone pit
  You are in a pit of old bones.
  Colours: $C2 $CE
  Look: The bones of those who came before. A shiver runs down your back.
