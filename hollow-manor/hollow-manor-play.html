Hollow Manor

ATARI 2600 · NTSC
arrows or WASD · space to fire · R resets · a gamepad works too

How to play

Push the stick toward a direction and press fire to walk that way. Fire on its own is LOOK: it shows what is here and opens the action menu.

In the action menu, left and right choose GO, TAKE, GIVE, USE, TALK, INVENTORY, READ, OPEN, HINT; fire picks one. GIVE and USE then list what you carry.

Made with Adventure Cart Workshop. The cartridge file runs on a real 2600 or in any emulator such as Stella.
