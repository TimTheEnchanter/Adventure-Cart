The Wyrm of Ashfell

A text adventure
ATARI 2600 CARTRIDGENTSC · 8K

The story

A wyrm has come to Ashfell and sleeps in the cave past the river. The villagers are too frightened to sleep.

The village needs a hero, and only steel will scare a wyrm.

Getting started

With the console switched off, put the cartridge in and switch on. The title picture appears; press fire to read on, and fire on START to begin. The console's reset switch starts over from the title at any time.

Playing

Everything is done with the joystick and its button. The bar at the bottom of the screen shows what fire will do.

Walk
Push the stick toward a direction (NORTH, EAST, SOUTH or WEST shows in the bar) and press fire.
LOOK
Press fire without pushing the stick: you see what is here, and the action menu opens.
Choose
In the action menu, push left or right to step through the actions, and press fire to pick one.

Actions

GO
leave the action menu and carry on walking
TAKE
pick up something you have found
GIVE
hand over something you carry
USE
use something you carry, here
TALK
speak to whoever is here
INVENTORY
list what you carry
HINT
a hint about what to do here, if you get stuck

GIVE and USE open a list of what you carry: left and right choose, fire picks. Look carefully: some things only show themselves when you look.

Scoring

You earn points as you make progress. When you win, the bar at the bottom of the screen shows your score out of 30, by turns with THE END.

30+
Wyrm-chaser
0+
Village lad

Danger

Some places are deadly. If the bar shows TRY AGAIN, press fire to start the adventure again from the beginning.

Your map
