Title: The Wyrm of Ashfell
Cart: 8K
Start: Village square
Ends when: Wyrm gone

Village square
  You are in the village square.
  Look: Folk hurry by. A torch burns in a bracket by the well.
  Item: a torch (look)
  Take: You take the torch.
  Talk: An old woman says: "The wyrm sleeps past the river. Only steel will scare it."
  East: Smithy
  West: Mine
  North: Forest path

Smithy
  You are in the smithy.
  Look: The smith pokes a cold forge.
  Talk: "No coal, no fire, no sword. Fetch me coal!"
  Give some coal: The forge roars. The smith hammers out a fine sword for you! [sets Forge lit]
  Item: a sword
  Take: You take the sword.

Mine
  You are at the old mine.
  Look: Black lumps litter the ground.
  Item: some coal (look)
  Take: You pick up some coal.

Forest path
  You are on a forest path.
  Look: Bees buzz around a hollow oak. Honey drips down.
  Item: a honeycomb (look)
  Take: You ease out a honeycomb. Only one sting!
  North: River bridge
  West: Cliff edge

Cliff edge
  You slip on the wet rocks and fall!
  Ending: game over

River bridge
  You are at a stone bridge.
  Look: A grumpy troll sits on the bridge, licking his lips.
  Talk: "Me hungry. Me want something SWEET."
  Give a honeycomb: The troll gobbles it up and waves you across. [sets Troll fed]
  North: Cave mouth (locked until Troll fed: The troll blocks the bridge.)

Cave mouth
  You are at the mouth of a cave.
  Look: Bones and ash. Smoke drifts out.
  North: Wyrm's lair

Wyrm's lair
  You are in the wyrm's lair.
  Dark unless a torch: It is black as pitch. Something huge breathes.
  Colours: $40 $1E
  Look: A great red wyrm sleeps on a pile of gold.
  Use a sword: You raise the gleaming sword. The wyrm wakes, shrieks and flies far away! [sets Wyrm gone]
