Title: The Lost Mine
Start: Camp
Ends when: Gold found

Camp
  You are at a quiet camp by the river.
  Look: A tent flaps in the wind. A lamp hangs from a pole.
  Item: an old lamp (look)
  Take: You unhook the lamp.
  North: Trail

Trail
  A dusty trail climbs into the hills.
  Look: An old miner sits on a rock.
  Talk: "Bring me something to eat, friend."
  Give an apple: He munches happily. "The mine is open now." [sets Mine open]
  East: Orchard
  North: Mine (locked until Mine open: Planks bar the way.)

Orchard
  Apple trees grow in neat rows.
  Look: One apple is still on a branch.
  Item: an apple (look)
  Take: You pick the apple.

Mine
  It is pitch dark down here.
  Dark unless an old lamp: You can't see a thing.
  Look: Gold glints in the rock!
  Dig: You dig out a nugget of gold! [needs an old lamp] [sets Gold found]
