Kestrel Station

A text adventure
ATARI 2600 CARTRIDGENTSC · 16K

The story

You wake from cold sleep aboard Kestrel Station. The crew is gone, the alarms are wailing, and the power is failing.

Find a way to the last escape pod before it is too late.

Getting started

With the console switched off, put the cartridge in and switch on. The title picture appears; press fire to read on through the opening pages, and fire on START to begin. The console's reset switch starts over from the title at any time.

Playing

Everything is done with the joystick and its button. The bar at the bottom of the screen shows what fire will do.

Walk
Push the stick toward a direction (NORTH, EAST, SOUTH or WEST shows in the bar) and press fire.
LOOK
Press fire without pushing the stick: you see what is here, and the action menu opens.
Choose
In the action menu, push left or right to step through the actions, and press fire to pick one.

Actions

GO
leave the action menu and carry on walking
TAKE
pick up something you have found
GIVE
hand over something you carry
USE
use something you carry, here
TALK
speak to whoever is here
INVENTORY
list what you carry
PRY
try it wherever it might help
SCAN
try it wherever it might help
REPAIR
try it wherever it might help
HINT
a hint about what to do here, if you get stuck
CODE
show a code for this point in the game

GIVE and USE open a list of what you carry: left and right choose, fire picks. Look carefully: some things only show themselves when you look.

Saving your place

The 2600 forgets everything when it is switched off, so CODE in the action menu shows a code for the room you are in, what you carry and what you have done. Write it down. Next time, choose CODE on the last title page and enter it letter by letter: left and right choose a letter, fire adds it, and DELETE takes one back.

Scoring

You earn points as you make progress. When you win, the bar at the bottom of the screen shows your score out of 60, by turns with THE END.

60+
Station commander
30+
Engineer
0+
Space cadet

Danger

Some places are deadly. If the bar shows TRY AGAIN, press fire to start the adventure again from the beginning.
